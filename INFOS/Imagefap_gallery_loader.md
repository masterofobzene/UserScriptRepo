### USE CASE:
--------------
Gives a simpler and easier UI by loading and showing all thumbnails like a booru style page so you don't have to
pick a gallery, see if the images are what you like, closing and opening another. This simply paginates all into one
infinite scroll page. It is integrated with the Gallery hider userscript, so you can hide trash and block users.

### USAGE:
---------------
Click on the "⚡ Booru Mode" button.

### WHY?:
---------------
With so many random content galleries, you don't know exactly whats inside by just looking at the first 4 preview
images that the site shows. This userscript presents all galleries contents (except manually blocked) in a "booru"
style page.
