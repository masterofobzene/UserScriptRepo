### USE CASE:
--------------
Common sense. The copy button now appears at the end of the message instead at the top, 
tiny microscopic scroll bars replaced with normal scroll bars.

### USAGE:
---------------
Load the page with this extension and it just works.

### WHY?:
---------------
I don't know what was the person behind the design of the page thinking when he/she thought that
placing the copy button on top was a good idea. You need to more or less see the actual AI answer
before blindingly copying it, this makes you have to scroll down (which was impossible due to
microscopic scroll bars and tiresome without mice with "free" scrollwheels) and back up again because the 
copy button was on top. Now you can take a peek at the code and at the end you will find the "copy" button.
